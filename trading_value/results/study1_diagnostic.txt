corr( forecast D(t) , HAR D(t) ) on eligible bars = 0.9542
corr( sqrt V_forecast , sqrt V_HAR )              = 0.9601
mean |forecast−HAR| / HAR   (relative gap)        = 11.65%
fraction of matched trades whose EXIT differs      = 89.51%
per-trade PnL diff (forecast−naive): mean=+0.134$  std=19.066$  p5=-15.30  p95=+17.14
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
per-trade PnL diff t-stat (illustrative) = 0.69  (n=9627)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTERPRETATION: the forecast changes the exit on 90% of trades (arms are NOT
identical), but the per-trade differences are DIRECTIONLESS NOISE (mean approx 0 vs large
std, t approx 0). The forecast reshuffles which trades win/lose without a SYSTEMATIC edge
over HAR: its small (+2.3% QLIKE) informational advantage does not translate into economic
value through stop/target adaptation.
Also: the base strategies are themselves unprofitable (all arms negative Sharpe), so there
is little positive edge for the forecast to modulate. The hold-out remains LOCKED.
